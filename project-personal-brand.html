What I set out to do

A personal brand, not another portfolio. My outline promised the basic aspects of a personal brand: my goals, my virtues and strengths beyond my degree, the services I would offer, and a tiny logo that represents me. It is fully digital, as a small experience built with Figma Design and Figma Make and as a section of this website.

I will have reached the goal if someone reading or trying it can clearly tell it is different from a portfolio: I am the subject, with my criteria, my values and my personality, in an analytical, narrative and conversational tone.

What I have so far

  • Strength

    I turn ideas into deliverables

    I break an idea down into deliverables, with an execution plan, a timeline, final delivery checklists and a pitch outline.

  • Strength

    I use AI with critical thinking

    I understand the logic of the topic first, then decide where, why and how to use AI.

  • Strength

    Persistence and self-directed research

    When I don't know something, I research until I know how to reach the goal.

  • Strength

    I organise and lead teams

    I coordinate, delegate, spot gaps early and question the design decisions being made.

  • Strength

    Rigour and honesty

    I analyse data critically and say clearly what it can and cannot show.

  • Strength

    I make complex ideas clear

    I think from the point of view of the average person, not only of the people who master the technical side.

The services I would offer. My main service is UX/UI and prototyping: I can structure the flow of an idea into an interactive prototype in Figma, validate it with usability testing and turn what I observe into concrete recommendations, so a team or client can see their idea in action before investing in development. My second service is coordination and planning for interactive projects: the execution plan, the timeline and each person's responsibilities, followed through to delivery. I am still working out the details of these services.

What comes next
