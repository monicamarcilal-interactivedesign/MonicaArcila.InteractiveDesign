El Feeling

E-commerce prototype, UI kit & brand manual · Reto Medialab 3 (Interfaces), Universidad EAFIT

My role
Team of 5. I did the information architecture and user needs, and designed the Classes section with its animation
Tools
Figma
Client (course project)
El Feeling, a roller-skate teacher's classes, custom gear and merch

The problem

El Feeling is run by a roller-skate teacher in Medellín. All of her content, including her classes, custom gear and merch, lived only on Instagram. Our real client brief was to design an e-commerce prototype that organised all of it clearly and made booking a class or buying something easy, while keeping the brand's existing tone.

My role

It was a team project with five of us, and I took on the structural side. I organised and analysed the information architecture, worked out the user's needs and how the services and products relate to each other, and helped with the project schedule. I defined interactions and card components for the UI kit as a base for the more visual side of the team to refine. I designed the whole Classes section, with its animation and responsive version, and helped put the final prototype together.

Process

We gathered the client's requirements and surveyed Medellín's roller-skate community. One teammate skates in her classes, which helped. The survey showed us who we were designing for:

  • Mostly women aged 17–26, studying and working, and comfortable paying online.
  • They use their phones most, so the site had to be fully responsive.
  • Instagram and TikTok are their main channels, so they expect visual, quick, direct content.
  • They have a strong sense of community and see skating as emotional release and personal growth.

From there we benchmarked brands like Cloudworldwide, WhyNot and Clap Burgers, then built the information architecture, a brand manual that extends her existing identity, and a UI kit covering the index, layout grid, desktop and mobile type scales, buttons, cards and navigation.

Solution

A clickable e-commerce prototype in Figma, built on the information architecture, brand manual and UI kit. It includes a Classes page for desktop and mobile and a pop-up booking flow, so someone can go from discovering a class to booking it without leaving the page.

Result & reflection

We met the project's goals. The prototype meets the client's needs and expands the brand's look without losing its identity. The site reflects the skating community's values, and buying isn't its only goal. A video of the prototype in use survives.
