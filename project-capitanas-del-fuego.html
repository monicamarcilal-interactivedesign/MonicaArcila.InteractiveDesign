Capitanas del Fuego

Interactive audiovisual experience for a Dancehall & Sunset party · Programación Creativa, Universidad EAFIT

My role
Team of 3. I developed the final visuals with my teammates' input and presented them to the client
Tools
TouchDesigner · Kinect · webcam
Client (course project)
A DJ based in Medellín, given to us through the course
  • Context

    Creative Programming at Universidad EAFIT. A client project for a Medellín DJ, in a team of three.

  • Challenge

    Background visuals for her Dancehall & Sunset party, built in TouchDesigner, with a narrative, and controllable live.

  • Solution

    Audio-reactive scenes the DJ switches from the keyboard, plus a camera filter that turns the crowd into digital fire.

  • Result

    I presented the visuals to the DJ myself, with a pitch deck and a recording of the full experience. She saw it, but it did not meet her expectations.

  • Learning

    We had to use our full potential in TouchDesigner. A good result for the course was not as good for the actual client.

The brief

Visuals for a party, in the DJ's hands. A Medellín DJ needed background visuals for her Dancehall & Sunset party. We had to use TouchDesigner, develop a narrative and build visuals she could control live.

My role

I developed the final visuals, with my teammates' input, in a team of three. I presented them to the DJ and showed her the visuals.

Process

  1. Learn the tool

    Before the client project, we taught ourselves TouchDesigner through YouTube tutorials and the lecturer's resources, learning how its node logic works.

  2. Three experiments

    Visuals that react to audio. A version where hand movements picked up by a Kinect drive the visuals. And one that uses the computer's camera so a face becomes part of the image.

  3. The concept

    We used AI only to shape a rough first narrative for the pitch, then adapted it ourselves: the DJs are flight captains, the crowd is the crew and dancehall is the engine. Fire, sunset aeroplanes and female silhouettes celebrate women's energy and empowerment.

  4. The visuals

    Audio-reactive scenes the DJ can switch from the keyboard, from beat-driven fire particles to warm-toned aeroplanes and an explosion of gold light for the climax.

Decisions

  • Decision

    The crowd becomes part of the show

    We designed a camera filter that turns the crowd's silhouettes into digital fire, so guests become part of the visuals and can film themselves in front of the screen.

  • Decision

    The DJ stays in control

    Scenes change from the keyboard, so she can play with them live during her set.

  • Decision

    AI for a first draft only

    I used AI to develop a rough first narrative for the pitch. We took what we needed and adapted it to our own presentation. The programming was learnt and built by us.

Result

  • 3people on the team
  • 3experiments before the client project: audio, Kinect, camera
  • 1presentation to the DJ, by me

I presented the visuals to the DJ myself. The final delivery was the pitch deck and a recording of the full experience running, which shows the whole result.

The DJ saw it, but it did not meet her expectations. By the course's terms and expectations it was good work. For the real client it was not as good as it could have been.

What we learnt: we had to use our full potential in TouchDesigner. We could have done it better for the actual client.
