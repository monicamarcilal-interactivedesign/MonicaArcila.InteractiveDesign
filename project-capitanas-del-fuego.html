Capitanas del Fuego

Interactive audiovisual experience for a Dancehall & Sunset party · Programación Creativa, Universidad EAFIT

My role
Team of 3. I developed the final visuals and presented them to the client
Tools
TouchDesigner · Kinect · webcam
Client (course project)
A DJ based in Medellín, given to us through the course

The brief

A Medellín DJ needed background visuals for her Dancehall & Sunset party. We had to use TouchDesigner, develop a narrative and build visuals she could control live.

Process

Before the client project, we taught ourselves TouchDesigner through tutorials and the lecturer's resources, learning how its node logic works. Our experiments included visuals that react to audio, a version where hand movements picked up by a Kinect drive the visuals, and one that uses the computer's camera so a face becomes part of the image.

For the concept, we used AI only to shape a rough first narrative for the pitch, then adapted it ourselves. The result: the DJs are flight captains, the crowd is the crew and dancehall is the engine. Fire, sunset aeroplanes and female silhouettes celebrate women's energy and empowerment.

Solution

A set of audio-reactive scenes the DJ can switch from the keyboard during her set, from beat-driven fire particles to warm-toned aeroplanes and an explosion of gold light for the climax. We also designed a camera filter that turns the crowd's silhouettes into digital fire, so guests become part of the visuals and can film themselves in front of the screen.

Result

I presented the visuals to the DJ myself. The final delivery was the pitch deck and a recording of the full experience running.

Tools & software
