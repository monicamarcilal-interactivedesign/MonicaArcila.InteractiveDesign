Botcito for Parque Explora

Mobile app prototype · Design Thinking coursework · Parque Explora, Medellín

My role
Team of 5 or 6. UI/UX design & gamification concept
Subject (course project)
Parque Explora, a science park in Medellín. Our teacher recommended it; we had no contact with the park
Tools
Figma
Timeframe
End of June to end of November 2023, one of my first projects
  • Context

    A Design Thinking course project (2023) about a real place I have visited since I was a child: Parque Explora, a science park in Medellín. Team of 5 or 6.

  • Challenge

    Make everyday visitor tasks feel like part of the park's identity, not generic app screens.

  • Solution

    A mobile companion around the park's own robot mascot, Botcito, with three features that all start with a QR scan.

  • Result

    A full, screen-by-screen Figma prototype covering three real visitor needs.

  • Learning

    One character and one entry point can tie unrelated tasks into one experience.

The problem

Ordinary tasks, handled with plain menus and signs. Parque Explora has practical visitor needs: ordering food at the cafeteria, finding which exhibit rooms suit a visitor, and sorting waste correctly afterwards.

The brief was to design a mobile companion around Explora's own robot mascot, Botcito, that makes those tasks feel like part of the park's identity.

My role

UI flows and gamification concepts, in a team. I designed the flows and the gamification across three features: the cafeteria ordering flow, the "what kind of explorer are you?" personality quiz, and the waste-sorting guide.

How a visit works

  1. Order food

    Scan a code at a table and a simple menu of the park's vendors opens (Mimo's, El Machetico, Dogger, Kon Food and others), each with its items and prices. No flagging anyone down.

  2. Find your room

    "¡Descubre qué explorador eres!" is a short quiz that gives each visitor one of four robot archetypes, each tied to a room in the park. Wayfinding becomes "which robot are you?".

  3. Play with Botcito

    A short narrative mini-game carries the park's origin story: Botcito, born in his workshop, sets out to create four worlds inside a new universe, the city of Medellín. The player guides him through dialogue choices and a side-scrolling level, and a two-player button lets a second visitor join.

  4. Sort your waste

    After a meal, scan a code Botcito shows and get an interactive tray (napkins, scraps, bottles, receipts, cutlery, ice, cardboard) to sort into organic, recyclable, non-recyclable inorganic or liquid waste. A quick, correctable lesson instead of a poster nobody reads.

The four explorer archetypes

Neurito (analytical, curious, drawn to questions) goes to the Mind Room. Mystique (creative, expressive, experimental) to the Stage Room. Tempu (patient, observant, detail-oriented) to the Time Room. Tamborin (passionate, curious about music) to the Music Room.

Decisions

  • Decision

    One character for everything

    Botcito and one visual system tie together three unrelated tasks, so the app reads as part of the park, not three separate tools.

  • Decision

    The same low-friction entry point

    Each feature starts with a QR scan, which keeps the interaction simple and mobile-first.

  • Decision

    Personality as wayfinding

    Mapping each personality to a room turns "where should I go?" into something visitors want to answer.

Result & reflection

  • 3real visitor needs covered
  • 4explorer archetypes, one per room
  • 4waste categories in the sorting guide

A full, screen-by-screen prototype about a real institution, built around one mascot and one gamification idea: a personality-to-room mapping that makes wayfinding more memorable than a directory.
