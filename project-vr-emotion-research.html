"VR Simulations to Elicit Emotion Impact on Cognitive Task Execution" · Research seedbed, Universidad EAFIT · ongoing

Tools

Status

Context
Research seedbed project alongside my degree, not part of any course

How I got here

After I presented Hansel & Gretel, my lecturer invited me to join his research seedbed, a group of students working on real research with staff. I joined as a developer on an ongoing study into how emotions triggered in VR affect how well people carry out cognitive tasks.

What I built

Haptics. I added haptic feedback to the VR simulation, so participants feel as well as see what happens.

Onboarding. I designed the tutorial that introduces participants to the experience. My first version was a separate tutorial. We later changed it so participants learn inside the experience itself, which kept them immersed.

Emotion check-in. I built a short in-app questionnaire with a face scale, from happy to sad. It records how participants feel before they enter the simulation and after they leave, which gives the researchers a baseline to compare against.

I worked on a shared GitHub project as the third main developer. The study has been through user testing, and the team is now analysing the results.

What's next

The seedbed has started a new project on digital avatars in Unreal Engine, where I'm in charge of researching and developing. It has only just begun, so there's nothing to show yet.
