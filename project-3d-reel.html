3D modelling, basic rigging & animation · Maya

Modelling: solo coursework. Basic rigging and animation: coursework exercises. Final animation: a team of six, where I shared the lighting with one teammate, rendered half of it (a teammate rendered the other half) and made the sound and music with another teammate
Tools

Format
Skills showcase from two courses: 3D Modelling, then 3D Animation the next semester
  • Context

    3D coursework at Universidad EAFIT in Maya, across two courses: 3D Modelling, then 3D Animation the next semester. A showcase of range, not a single brief.

  • Challenge

    Grow from simple objects to detailed models, then to rigging and animation.

  • Solution

    A modelling progression I made alone, rigging and animation exercises, and a team animation where I shared the lighting with one teammate, rendered half of it and made the sound and music with a teammate.

  • Result

    A modelling portfolio (May 2025) that ends in an original character, and a finished animation made by a team of six.

  • Learning

    Each stage added a skill on top of the last.

What this is

Range across the 3D pipeline. This is not one client brief. It is a body of 3D coursework built up in stages, from simple objects to detailed models, then to basic rigging and animation.

My role

  • Alone

    Modelling (3D Modelling course)

    The modelling progression, from primitives to detailed models and an original character, was solo coursework.

  • Exercises

    Basic rigging and animation (3D Animation course)

    Character rigging and simple movement cycles, as coursework exercises.

  • Team of six

    Final animation: lighting, rendering, sound and music

    In our team animation, La llegada de Dizo, one teammate and I shared the lighting, a teammate and I split the rendering in half, and I made the sound and music with another teammate.

The progression

  1. From the simple…

    Basic primitives and low-detail objects first, like a chair set and a barrel.

  2. …to the complex

    Models with real surface detail, believable proportions and clean topology, as the skills built on each other.

  3. Texturing

    Textured characters, including four astronaut-style figures.

  4. A character from idea to reality

    Bloom, known as Bloomie, a two-legged "animal" of the species Nectidali Raflorie. It creates and carries the nutrients missing from the Amazon's plants, to help the forest regrow.

The animation

A finished team animation, made by six of us. My contribution was part of the lighting, half of the rendering, and the sound and music (with another teammate). The gallery shows the title card, the landscape, the star-shaped creature and the night scene, and the video plays the animation.
