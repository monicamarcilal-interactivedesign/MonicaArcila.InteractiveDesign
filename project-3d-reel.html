What this is

Not a single client brief, but a body of 3D coursework built up in stages — starting from simple objects, moving through more detailed models, and on to basic rigging and animation. It's here to show range across the 3D pipeline rather than one problem and one solution.

Progression

The modelling work moves deliberately from simple to complex — basic primitives and low-detail objects first, building toward models with real surface detail, believable proportions and clean topology as the skills compounded. The animation side starts with basic character rigging and simple movement cycles.

What's in the reel

A modelling progression (made on my own), basic rigging and animation exercises, and a final team animation, La llegada de Dizo, made by six of us. In that team animation my part was the lighting, the rendering, and the sound and music.
