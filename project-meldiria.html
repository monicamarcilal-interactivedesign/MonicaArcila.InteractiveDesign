Meldiria

Narrative universe & 2D videogame · Universos Narrativos + Animación 2D + Lenguajes de Programación, Universidad EAFIT · 2024

Award
Most Creative Project, Inventiva 2024
My role
Team of 6. I led the world-building (Mythos, Ethos, Topos, the world bible) and the game development
Tools
Unity · AI-assisted scripting and brainstorming
Deliverables
World bible, 2D videogame, 2D animation, artbook, personality quiz
  • Context

    Three EAFIT courses (Narrative Universes, 2D Animation, Programming Languages) combined into one project by a team of six.

  • Challenge

    Invent a world solid enough that a game, an animation, an artbook and a quiz could all come from it without contradicting each other.

  • Solution

    A world bible (Mythos, Ethos, Topos), a "rule of three" that links every element, and a 2D platformer-puzzle I developed in Unity.

  • Result

    Nominated at Inventiva, EAFIT's creativity and technology showcase, and won Most Creative Project 2024.

  • Learning

    My first game as lead developer. It's the project that made me want to keep building games.

The problem

One universe had to feed many products. At EAFIT you can combine three courses into one project, or make three separate pieces. We chose one, because a shared universe gives you more value for the same time.

So Meldiria had to hold up as more than a setting for one game. It needed its own physics, social structure and rules, specific enough that every deliverable could be built from it without contradicting itself.

My role

Narrative designer and lead developer in a team of six. Teammates who love animation led the 2D animation. I focused on two things.

The world.

  • Led the writing of the Mythos, Ethos and Topos, which define how Meldiria's characters behave in their own contexts.
  • Built the "rule of three": linking every narrative element to others, so even the smallest detail makes sense inside the world.
  • Proposed new, Meldiria-specific names for the three sections.
  • Wrote, checked and laid out the full world bible, the Biblia de Mundo: numbered, in order, with a clear hierarchy.
  • Used AI to brainstorm and to find gaps in my logic, but every choice had a reason I could explain.

The game.

  • My first time as a developer. I took the lead, programming it alongside one teammate so I could take breaks.
  • Wrote the scripts and checked every mechanic against our project outline.
  • Asked teammates for the assets, animations and interfaces we needed.
  • Debugged with our lecturer, online research and AI, and made the final build.

Building the world

  1. Mythos · who and why

    The Meldirianos are split into four social roles (architects, farmers, artisans, guardians), each moving through three life stages (child, adult, sage) across 100 to 120 years. Their enemy is Dizo, a cosmic-horror jester in CMYK colours who drags people into an incomprehensible fourth mental dimension.

    A legend says an ocarina hides the key to decoding Dizo's "ecoires" (echo-waves). It sends a farmer-explorer through the biomes, predators and Dizo's interference, a structure that doubles as the game's level progression.

  2. Ethos · how they behave

    Values, spoken and unspoken: community before the individual, perseverance, loyalty, cyclical renewal. Down to game-ready rules: guardians must hand over the magic dust honestly or face a sanction, and every sanction aims to restore balance and stay proportionate.

  3. Topos · where it happens

    Eight physical settings, from the central plaza and its Tree of Birth to the sound-dead plains full of predators and the Aurelis range, where magic-making winds blow every six months. Light is replaced by "ecoire" waves, driven by a black sun and a moon's gravity, which create named weather like windy storms, inverse rain and velvet lakes.

  4. Systems · so every scene has rules

    Rights, obligations and sanctions. Four ruling councils (Sages, Guardians, Artisans, Farmers) plus a community assembly. Guided excursions from age 15 to 25 as education. A harvest inventory that decides how food is shared out.

Why I renamed Mythos, Ethos and Topos

I proposed names from music, so they would fit Meldiria's world of sound waves. La Clave Tiempacte for the Mythos: a "clave" is the clef at the start of a score that tells you how to read it, like the story's starting point, and "Tiempacte" joins time, actors and facts. I sent the group three options with a reason for each, plus alternatives that were not musical.

The game

"La ocarina perdida" is a 2D platformer-puzzle about perception. You play Do, a farmer. The world is black and white, and only Dizo's CMYK waves have colour. You solve puzzles by reading those waves with sight and sound.

  • Decision

    A world without colour

    The Meldirianos can't see colour. They "see" by sensing waves with their antennae, so their world, and the game, is black and white. When Dizo arrives, a jester who wants to play with them, he gives them CMYK waves: the first colour they have ever known, and the strangest, most disruptive thing in their world.

  • Obstacle

    Solving bugs all the time

    It was my first important project in Unity, and programming it felt like fixing one bug after another. I stayed with it until each mechanic worked.

  • Decision

    Four levels, drawn from the world

    Each level draws on a place in the world, from the Forest in Reverse to the Suffocating Valleys and the lost temple. Two helpers can be played: an architect who can super-jump and an artisan who can shrink.

We first called the game "La llegada de Dizo", a provisional name we used to explain our idea to our 2D animation professor. This is how it was planned in those slides. The gameplay video shows what we built.

Solution

One structured world bible that every other piece draws from: the game's plot and levels, the artbook's visual language and a personality quiz that gives players one of Meldiria's own social roles (architect, farmer, artisan or guardian) instead of a generic result.

Result & reflection

  • 6people on the team
  • 3courses, one universe
  • 8physical settings in the Topos
  • 1award: Most Creative Project, Inventiva 2024

Meldiria was nominated at Inventiva and won Most Creative Project 2024. We presented the game, used the personality quiz to draw people in, and dressed up as Meldirianos, antennae included. We later collected everything the universe produced into a media portfolio, the "Mapa de Plataformas".

Some of my working documents are still visible drafts, with notes still to discuss and a glossary that isn't finished. That's how worldbuilding really works: knowing which parts of a system are still undecided is as much part of the method as the parts that are done.
